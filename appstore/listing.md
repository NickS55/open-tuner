- Description:
  Open Tuner is a simple, accurate tuner and metronome for any instrument — guitar, bass, ukulele, violin, cello, brass, woodwinds, or your voice.

  It's completely free. No ads popping up mid-rehearsal, no subscriptions, no account, no tracking. Open it and play.

  TUNER
  • Chromatic: detects any note from about 38 Hz to 2 kHz, low B on a bass through flute
  • Large note name and octave, readable from your music stand
  • Smooth cents needle that turns green when you're in tune
  • Adjustable reference pitch (A4 = 430–450 Hz)

  METRONOME
  • 30–260 BPM with slider, fine +/– buttons, and tap tempo
  • 1–7 beats per bar with an accented downbeat
  • Keeps time with the screen locked and plays alongside your music

  Your microphone audio is analyzed live on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere.

  Open Tuner is open source: github.com/NickS55/open-tuner
