# Open Tuner Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Open Tuner does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **Microphone:** The tuner uses your microphone only to detect the pitch of the note you're playing. Audio is analyzed live on your device and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.** The app makes no network connections.
- **Settings** (like your reference pitch) stay on your device.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/NickS55/open-tuner/issues
